Anything you draw in PaintPad is yours. PaintPad claims no licence over your artwork, and because drawings never leave your browser there is nothing for anyone else to hold a copy of. You do not need to credit PaintPad for your own work.

Running an unmodified hosted copy

You may use the hosted editor as-is. You do not need to publish anything, disclose that you used it, or offer your users any source, because you are not distributing a modified version.

Running your own hosted copy

This is the part of the AGPL that catches people out, and it is section 13 — the licence was extended specifically because of web applications.

  • If you run your own hosted copy unmodified, you may do so without obligation.
  • If you modify PaintPad and then let other users interact with your modified version over a network — which includes simply hosting it — you must offer those users the corresponding source of your modified version.
  • "Offer" means putting the source where those users can reach it, not merely being willing to send it if asked.

Using the hosted editor

The hosted editor is offered as-is and may be changed, interrupted or withdrawn at any time. Because your drawing lives only in your own browser's storage, clearing site data, clearing the drawing in the app, or a change to how the app stores documents will lose it. Export anything you want to keep.

Acceptable use

Do not use the hosted editor to distribute malware, to infringe someone else's rights, or to break the law. Because drawings are stored on your own device, the editor neither hosts nor has access to your content.
