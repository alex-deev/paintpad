Privacy

PaintPad has no server, no accounts and no analytics. Your drawing never leaves your browser, because there is nowhere for it to go.

The short version

  • No account, and nothing identifying you.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.
  • No cookies set by the editor.
  • No network requests to any server other than the one serving this page.
  • Your drawing is stored on your own device and you can delete it at any time.

What is stored, and where

The editor saves to your browser's own storage so your work survives a reload. There are exactly three places it writes to, all of them on your device and all of them readable and removable by you.

IndexedDB

Database paintpad
Object store documents, entry current. Holds one saved document, as a log of drawing operations rather than as an image. Reloading replays this log to rebuild the drawing. If you make changes and reload without them being written — which can happen if the tab closes mid-save — a copy is parked in localStorage under paintpad.flush and used on your next visit.

localStorage

paintpad.recents
Up to twelve recently used colours, stored as hex strings such as #1b9e77. Purely a convenience so recently used colours reappear. It is never read by anything else.
paintpad.flush
A deferred copy of the pending save, used only when IndexedDB is unavailable — private browsing modes, or a browser with site data disabled. It is overwritten on each save and removed once the save lands.

Deleting your data

Clearing site data for this site removes all of the above at once — the saved document, the recent colours and any pending save. There is no copy anywhere else, so this is permanent and unrecoverable.

You can also clear it from inside the editor: the Clear button in the toolbar erases the current drawing and its saved copy. Clearing recent colours additionally requires clearing site data.

Analytics and advertising

As of the time of writing, PaintPad runs no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, and loads no fonts, images or code from third-party hosts. The only network traffic is fetching the page and its own assets from the server that hosts them.

If advertising or analytics are ever added, this page has to be updated before they ship, and where the law requires it — the UK, the EU and Switzerland, for example — you will be asked for consent before anything is loaded.

Children

PaintPad is a general-audience drawing tool and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Because no data is transmitted, there is nothing to notify you about and no list of recipients to update.
