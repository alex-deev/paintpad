What to include in a bug report

The more of these you can give, the faster it can be reproduced:

  • What you did, and what you expected instead.
  • Your browser and version, and your operating system.
  • Whether you were drawing at a particular zoom level or canvas size.
  • Whether the drawing had autosaved — a bug that only appears after a reload usually points at replay rather than drawing.

Privacy enquiries

PaintPad collects no personal information, so there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you want your drawing removed, clear site data in your browser — see Privacy for exactly what is stored and where.

Security reports

If you believe you have found a security issue, please report it to the address above rather than opening a public issue, and give a reasonable amount of time for a fix before disclosing it.
